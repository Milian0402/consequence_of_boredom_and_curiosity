# Native x86-64 production acceptance

The eight layouts are fixed in [the stream-B panel](../stream/panel.csv), SHA-256 `466c2b51ee6df5fd737f3f4b1977c5b4ad75cdb7ae61bbaec7bc5919530f18fd`. The five rows named `fresh-*` were held out in the original experiment and are repeated for this acceptance run. The other three rows are diagnostics. Study seeds are 348271759 and 710438957. Use four whole-output FP64 fixtures per method and layout, finite absolute error at most 0.002, input and padding checks, one pinned process thread and a first-call observation for every fixture. Time three warm 32-call batches per seed in counterbalanced order, reversing the panel for the second block.

The generic production comparison uses the CMake Release archive with GCC's conservative x86-64 target in both builds. One uses its default configuration. The second sets only `COB_ENABLE_X86_STREAM_B=ON`. Each speedup is the median of six matching baseline/candidate time ratios. The five previously held-out layouts must reach a geometric mean of at least 1.03, positive geometric means in both seed blocks, at least four layout medians above one and a minimum layout median of 0.98.

The integration parity comparison uses the CMake Release archive with native tuning, the same dispatch switches as the original research baseline and streaming enabled. Compare it with the exact frozen research candidate, built using its original script at `b2cdc6a2985726cb1658dc7cc730b5a1c4b99680`. Require a five-layout geometric mean of at least 0.98 and a minimum of 0.95 for the research/integrated paired ratios. Report the tuned production off/on and frozen research incumbent/production-tuned-on comparisons separately.

All eight production public executables and their separate route witnesses are linked against the same respective archive. CMake executables use its installed static archive and header. For Make, assemble the unmodified objects produced by its normal test build into an audit archive. Test the conservative AT&T and Intel assembly dialects under both build systems. Hardware dispatch tests cover AVX2/FMA, available AVX-512F and the feature-masked fallback. The warmed field includes distro OpenBLAS, pinned ordinary sequential oneMKL and its JIT-capable direct-call interface, and pinned single-thread AOCL-BLAS 6.0.0. Report the fastest qualified included peer on every shape. The field and method labels identify these specific providers.

A timing gate failure is a measured outcome. Missing source, route or numerical proof invalidates acceptance. The benchmark records host, ISA, complete source revision, actual archive and worker identities, raw paired observations and first-call measurements before scoring.
