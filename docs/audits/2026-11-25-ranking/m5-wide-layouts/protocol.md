# Apple compact medium-wide head-to-head

The frozen domain is compact row-major FP32, 24–55 rows, 1024–4096 columns, 96–384 inner dimension, on the active 16-lane Apple SME2.1 opt-in. Fully charged public calls retain usual overlap/workspace checks. Compare opt-in COB at 602a9dddce7e940a282b0716a7b2ca40bd2ad35e and three different ways of feeding the existing arithmetic unit. Direct 16×64 visits B without any copy while looping over output columns first. Vertical strip tiles feed a 32–64-row block from 16 columns of original B, choosing three or four ZA tiles as needed. Superpanel packs four adjacent B panels per row and shares them across all A row tiles. Pre-existing opt-in path remains the fallback outside the domain. No shape-specific switches, numerical fixture detectors or persistent data caches.

Three already-timed compact cases 24×1280×131, 39×2304×160 and 55×1536×384 are the training set. Select the single candidate with the largest geometric mean of median incumbent/candidate times, across the three, using two training seeds and three rounds each. Record losses. Freeze selected source hash before viewing or timing the independent panel. Do not retune from the new targets. If no candidate beats the incumbent in training, test the leading candidate anyway and keep the baseline unless the same holdout rule passes.

For eight independently selected, previously unmeasured compact shapes and three unchanged inputs outside the gate, collect two seed blocks of four rotated warm trials each. Each public call pays all setup/packing/output costs. Measure actual public 602a9dd and candidate, explicitly serial Accelerate, prepared direct LIBXSMM, OpenBLAS, ArmPL, MaxMulSK and MpGEMM on supported compact shapes. Record every round, complete independent normalized FP64 checks and memory canaries. Inclusion requires one caller thread and valid output. The fastest direct peer is selected per shape by median absolute time; all candidate/peer ratios are computed using matched trials. First invocation in a new process is a separate score and includes JIT creation for LIBXSMM.

Promotion requires >=1.04× incumbent geometric mean, at least 6/8 above incumbent on each seed, >=0.95× incumbent in every compact holdout, >=1.01× fastest measured external peer geometric mean, at least 5/8 >1.01× that peer, and none <0.92× that peer. Correctness, guards and fallback must pass. Results falling short remain scratch candidates. Give kernel and method provenance. No claim covers other hardware, layouts, threads or peer ensembles.
