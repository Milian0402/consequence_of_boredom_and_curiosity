# Compact Apple SGEMM numerical challenge

Compare the installed public candidate against 602a9dd with COB_ENABLE_APPLE_MEDIUM_WIDE enabled. Bind cob_sgemm_rowmajor in each independently via dlopen and RTLD_FIRST. Optionally bind a separate candidate trace library's monotonically increasing uint64_t counter of completed new calls. Acceptance of the trace requires one selection on compact row-major layouts within 24–32 rows, 1024–4096 columns, and depth 96–384, and zero on all bypass controls. Traced and ordinary candidate outputs must match bit-for-bit.

Each logical backing allocation for A, B and C sits between two inaccessible guard pages. Runs alternately place the matrix's first float against the leading page or its last float against the trailing page. The compact cases retain natural lda=K, ldb=ldc=N even next to protected pages. Each input allocation is read-only during the public calls. C begins as NaN. Its row padding and all accessible bytes outside the matrix must retain a sentinel.

The fixtures include independent normalized random values, small exactly representable dyadics, near-cancelling pairs, reciprocal power-of-two factors with exponents from -80 to +80 across K, and correlated inputs multiplied by 16 and 32. Those scales intentionally reproduce finite outputs with absolute error above 0.002 in ordinary FP32. The historical 0.002 criterion applies to normalized, reciprocal and cancellation fixtures. Dyadics must be exact. For scaled fixtures, record the correctly rounded FP32 floor, the number of errors over 0.002 in each method and those newly introduced or removed. Any newly introduced failures require review. At every scale reject a result exceeding the conservative standard sequential FP32 forward-error bound gamma(2K) times the sum of absolute products, allowing for the FP64 reference's own error. Bypass controls require bit-identical results to the incumbent.

Run after native timing ends. No timers or tuning commands are present in the verifier.

A separate trace build is compiled with an explicit one-shot allocation fault in the SME leaf translation unit. The first otherwise eligible call must hit the fault once, bypass the candidate, and complete every exact-dyadic output via ordinary COB. The next call must succeed and increment the trace counter. Nine invalid/no-op argument calls must leave the entire sentinel output unchanged. Four exact/partial output overlaps with A or B must bypass the counted route and reproduce the full incumbent backing buffer. Overlap behavior is compared directly to the incumbent without assigning a mathematical product to an unsupported alias.

The narrow guard tests route six compact shapes ranging from 24 to 32 rows. The 33-row boundary and historical 39- and 55-row compact cases must retain the prior implementation. All three independent stride-only bypass controls use an otherwise eligible 28-row shape. The six eligible layouts exercise two protected boundaries and six numerical fixtures, with a separate shared-input case, for 74 eligible calls. Twelve distinct bypass layouts at two boundaries produce 24 bypass calls. The 98-case numerical harness and 15 separate fallback cases contain no clock or throughput measurement.
