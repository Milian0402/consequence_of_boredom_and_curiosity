# Certified single-product SGEMM

This prospective comparison uses one standard GitHub-hosted Linux x86-64 runner and one standard Linux Arm64 runner. Both allocations count regardless of CPU. The public FP32 entry computes row-major overwrite C = AB on disjoint, possibly strided FP32 A, B and C. Positive M,N and 1 ≤ K ≤ 4096 allow the content certificate. Both operands must contain exactly binary32 integers of magnitude ≤256 and K × max|A| × max|B| ≤ 2²⁴. Under this certificate the BF16 representation, every product and every possible intermediate FP32 partial sum are exact integers. Positive and negative zero are numerically equivalent. The pointer/stride assumptions and ISA-specific rounding argument are in dispatch/proof.md. NaNs, infinities, fractions, outside magnitudes, excessive depth, an unsafe bound, or unavailable hardware select the ordinary FP32 provider. Its own rounding and exceptional-value behavior apply. No approximation passes the content gate.

The selected BF16 engine is the published, unchecked one-product adapter. Intel AMX or x86 AVX512-BF16 requires a full matching-dimension execution, an eligible oneDNN descriptor and BF16 dot instructions in the arithmetic JIT dump. Arm uses Arm Compute Library's real Advanced SIMD BFMMLA microkernel with direct symbol/packing witnesses. The isolated benchmark supplies each shape's proven hardware availability to the dispatcher at initialization, before timing. Bounds and data-content checks happen in every ordinary call. No inputs, certificates, converted bytes, packing or output data are cached. A branch inside the FP32 fallback chooses the prior large near-square leader for matrices whose three dimensions are all ≥4096 and whose aspect ratio is ≤2. On x86 this is released COB aligned two-level Winograd with sequential oneMKL. On Arm this is FastMMW with threshold 1536. Otherwise use the sequential oneMKL JIT-capable public call on x86 and ArmPL on Arm. Both underlying providers and the identical fallback entry are measured independently on the same inputs. The research adapter exposes benchmark plugin initialization and accepts nonoverlapping views. General production integration would also need arbitrary-shape dynamic hardware admission and the caller's API requirements.

One pinned thread performs six matched repetitions, two seeds × three balanced orderings. A fresh process times its first complete multiply and its next warmed multiply.
