# Confirmation of standalone ragged HK15 against released Winograd

The fixed primary is the ordinary standalone `ragged-hk15` public SGEMM. The original N2 experiment measured an advantage over independent libraries on three new ragged matrices, then lacked all four released vendor-backed Winograd workers on that CPU. This experiment repeats those three matrix views with new input seeds, compares all four released workers, and adds three previously unmeasured views. The historical experiments motivate the registration and receive no weight in the new timings. ## Methods and roles

The warmed one-thread operation overwrites C with row-major FP32 A×B. Use native optimization flags identically for all paired COB baselines. Links, compiler options, runtime CPU, core selection, source tree, software versions and binary digests belong with each result.

* `ragged-hk15` uses COB's own child kernels. The pre-ragged standalone `released-hk15` is a diagnostic incumbent.
* The released Winograd field includes exactly four mandatory public CMake configurations per host. OpenBLAS and the platform provider each run both ordinary two-level and aligned two-level Winograd. The platform provider is ArmPL on AArch64 and sequential oneMKL on x86. Charge all temporary allocation, leaf work and fringe updates inside the timed public call.
* Two fixed secondary HK candidates substitute whole serial BLAS leaf products. They are `hk15-openblas-leaf` and `hk15-armpl-leaf` or `hk15-mkl-leaf` on the respective host. Report each separately against standalone HK15, its own direct provider, the corresponding released Winograd and the full field. These variants never replace the registered primary. The claim denominator stays fixed when an optional candidate is unavailable.
* Independent one-shot methods include serial OpenBLAS, BLASFEO, both FastMMW leaf cutoffs (768 and 1536), and native ArmPL or oneMKL, the oneMKL direct/JIT-capable interface and AOCL as appropriate. On x86 also enter the explicit oneMKL handles at `mkl-handle-oneshot` (handle creation in the call) and `mkl-handle-prepared` (retained handle, first-call setup observed). Each layout must show a native generated kernel or a clearly labeled library fallback, verified address/geometry and observed create/execute counts. An unavailable or rejected handle stays disclosed in the relevant tier. A measured fallback is separately identified from native code generation. Distinguish both from the direct/JIT-capable interface. Record actual qualifying oneDNN one-shot and cached-plan implementations separately, including their primitive counts. Retain TBLIS entries if the installed CPU backend qualifies. On Arm also enter optional independent one-shot `blis` (pinned serial upstream BLIS) and `acl` (fresh Arm Compute Library FP32 GEMM operator) when their native kernels and strides qualify. Other verified retained methods enter the all-method field with their roles and availability recorded. A library with setup, packing or workspace inside its one-shot interface pays that cost each call. Report retained-plan warm and first-call results with their plan semantics.
* A claim against the strongest measured field requires both standalone builds, all four released Winograd configurations, the architecture's direct peers, both FastMMW cutoffs and BLASFEO to qualify on each scored layout. The widest field also requires the previously measured oneDNN cached-plan method. A built optional oneDNN one-shot implementation participates and requires per-layout numerical qualification for a complete one-shot field claim. Report every known omission and failed admission. Any qualifying optional HK, TBLIS or additional independent implementation can win and can defeat a primary claim.

## Correctness, routing and timing

Before timing, establish that the four mandatory released Winograd methods actually link to the pinned serial libraries and complete a public hot-path trace. Confirm the exact number, dimensions, coefficients and boundary calls for their respective schedules. Both HK-leaf variants must issue the fifteen registered core products to the named provider. The standalone HK15 trace must show its own fifteen child products on each layout, with actual right, bottom and depth remainders. Compare normal and instrumented full outputs at fixed input. The earlier standalone's ineligible ragged path, any admission failure, and every observed first-call/worker outcome have explicit evidence. A genuine numerical exclusion remains visible in the report.

Use random seeds 197906831 and 884039527, cancellation at the first seed and split-scale at the second. Verify every output against the cached serial FP64 reference, using maximum absolute error ≤ 0.002, finite values, unmodified A/B and unchanged C padding. Reuse each golden across all methods and rounds after binding its logical input hashes, shape, mode and seed. Recheck 23 deterministic scalar double dot products per golden. The full row-major FP64 comparison includes every border and corner. Capture the observed pointer residues, caller strides, cutoff/leaf counts, actual threads and CPU. A worker must operate as one Linux task pinned to one allowed logical CPU. A failed method cannot contribute times on that shape.

For each shape/method/round use a fresh process after library initialization. Capture one first call, then one timed complete warm call on the same data. Three counterbalanced rounds per seed supply six warm pairs. Reverse shape and method sequences in the second seed block and keep the actual ordinal positions. Store wall and caller CPU durations. Preserve four accuracy runs and six timed calls, plus raw numerical errors and exclusions. First-call results are secondary.

## Scoring

Select the qualified competitor with the lowest raw warm median for each layout and comparison tier. For a candidate, exclude only that candidate when selecting its rival. The following tiers remain separate: direct libraries and independent one-shot libraries, independently authored implementations including retained plans, released COB with BLAS leaves, all one-shot methods, and every eligible method including retained plans and optional HK variants. Preserve the fastest raw method including the candidate and the runner-up. Report matched time differences in milliseconds (peer minus candidate) and speed ratios (peer divided by candidate), all six round outcomes, both seed-block medians, geometric mean across layouts, and the range of medians after dropping each one of the six pairs. Use host-specific data throughout.

For a given host, each of the repeat and fresh cohorts has its own three-shape test. The primary wins a cohort against a tier if every shape has (1) full required coverage, (2) strictly lower raw median than every admitted rival, (3) a paired median ratio strictly above 1.02, and (4) at least five wins out of six against that shape's fastest qualified rival. The geometric means of the three within-seed median ratios must also exceed one in each seed block. An exact tie is recorded and does not count as a win. To confirm leadership in this scoped N2 test, the fixed standalone method must pass the all-method tier on both cohorts, all six layouts. The independent-only result and the matched old-standalone comparison remain separately reported. The seventh, historical anchor stays out of all gates.

Apply the same tests separately to the x86 host. A new hardware family requires its own admissible result. A cross-architecture claim requires passage on both actual architectures. Secondary provider HK candidates use the same comparisons, separately labeled by provider, without substituting the faster of the two. Six paired measurements on one host offer limited resolution. Report the observed magnitude, leave-one-pair-out stability and numerical headroom. Neither a small single-host lead nor six positive observations establishes broad hardware or shape generality.
