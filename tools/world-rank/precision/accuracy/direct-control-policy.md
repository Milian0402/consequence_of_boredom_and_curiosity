# Supplemental control comparison

The original input register and gamma-K contract remain in registration.json. This comparison was specified after the original serial-FMA test-double run and before inspecting revised range-rescue or hardware results. Numerical cutoffs are operational choices for a demanding comparator. They do not establish a floating-point error theorem.

For each normal case and each seed, compute the candidate's maximum absolute error and root mean square absolute error from the exact dots across the entire logical matrix. Compute the corresponding pair for the actual direct FP32 implementation on the identical inputs and compare like with like. The companion direct-quality screen requires the candidate maximum and RMS to be at most the larger of twice the actual direct error and twice the matching correctly rounded half-ULP budget, on every normal matrix. Define that per-output budget by h=max(2^-150, 2^(floor(log2|P|)-24)) for exact nonzero P and h=2^-150 for zero. The maximum budget is max(h) and the RMS budget is sqrt(mean(h²)). At an exact power of two the upper-binade spacing applies. The comparison uses integer maxima and squared sums, avoiding ratio rounding and scale-dependent epsilons. A case whose direct output was exact still has a small representational allowance. Missing or invalid control output makes the verdict unavailable. The relative factor 2 was chosen for interpretability. Passing this secondary screen and the primary gates is required for a matched-direct-FP32 headline. Absolute and relative differences *between the two implementations* are also reported for each case. Relative RMS difference divides their RMS difference by the RMS direct result, and relative maximum divides their largest absolute difference by the largest direct output magnitude. Zero denominators are identified; maxima from near-zero individual references are recorded separately from these matrix-scale measures.

The overall accuracy status requires complete coverage, all normal-case output classes correct and every normal dot inside gamma-K, exact rounding on the three representable HK families, zero overwrite on zero-depth, and correct class on every range and exceptional output. Each criterion is exposed separately. The independent direct-quality screen is reported alongside this result. Implementation admission and hardware claims also require route evidence assessed by the native harness.
