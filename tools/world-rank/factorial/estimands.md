# Registered stride and start-offset effects

This experiment changes six features of the backing storage of the same FP32 matrices. Each feature has two levels. The six mask bits, low bit first, change the leading dimensions of A, B and C and then the independent starting offsets of A, B and C. The reported time is microseconds per complete call after warm-up. Qualification and startup times stay separate.

For each geometry, implementation and matched seed/round, take the natural log of each of the 64 whole-call times. For any factor, compute 32 differences of high minus low log time with the other five bits held fixed. Average these differences equally and exponentiate. A factor ratio above one means raising that factor increased the elapsed time. The per-geometry point estimate averages all six matched round contrasts on the log scale. The reader can inspect all six round estimates, both three-round seed estimates and ranges.

For each of the 15 distinct pairs, calculate the four-corner log difference of differences, high-high minus high-low minus low-high plus low-low. Average over the 16 configurations of the remaining four factors, then average equally across the six rounds. The exponentiated result is a ratio of ratios. Above one means raising one factor increased the time ratio associated with raising the other. Report all 15, with the three within-operand stride-by-origin interactions prespecified for interpretation. Results from different geometries and implementations remain distinct. These ratios describe this factorial and have no workload-population confidence level.

Compare mask 63 directly with mask 0 within each round; report that endpoint ratio and the exact historical comparison when its geometry, masks, CPU and public operation match. A main-effects-only sum need not reproduce this corner comparison. Also calculate the same matched main effects and interactions for the stream/oneMKL time ratio, to identify which layout changes alter their *relative* gap. Retain six round contrasts, two seed contrasts, the across-geometry sign count and the individual geometry ratios. No mechanism can be inferred solely from the direction of these timing differences.
